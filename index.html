Welcome to the Intro to Surgery DeCal at UC Berkeley!

Practical, workshop-based training in fundamental surgical skills. We start with basic techniques and work our way up, from tying a knot to performing an anastomosis.

No experience needed.

No prior background knowledge is necessary, and all majors are welcome. If surgery interests you, this class is for you.
